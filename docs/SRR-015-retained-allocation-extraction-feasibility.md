## Proposed single title-card observation

This proposal applies once the preconditions have been met and reviewed.
The approval boundary is one authenticated Steam-launched process epoch
at the responsive title card, one pair of read-only collections, with
the operator holding the checkpoint through the pair. Freeze the exact
reader source, test/fixture identities, budget configuration, independent
expected manifest, comparison classification version, executable/source
pin, admitted load-order policy and evidence destinations. Run the
offline fixtures and replay check before seeking that approval. Use the
four [historical samples](SRR-012-sample-plan.md) as predeclared regression
controls under their applicable inputs. A qualified control discrepancy
is a result to retain, never a reason to hide other rows.

1. Reuse the [SRR-013 run-card controls](SRR-013-structural-title-run-card.md)
   appropriate to ordinary process-read access. Record executable and
   mapped-image identity, operator checkpoint, Steam attribution,
   process creation identity, data/plugin provenance and documented
   environment and save protections. Arrange and verify any required
   protective setup under the later approval. Keep evidence and backup
   custody private. Recheck available space for projected evidence and
   use a fresh append-only evidence destination.
2. Open a least-rights query/read handle for the authenticated epoch.
   Start each pass at publication and the registry. Count work for
   headers, branch/leaf/block visits, descriptors, FormID chain reads,
   boundary rereads and journal bytes. Set ceilings from independently
   qualified layouts, admitted data volume and disposable fixture
   measurements, with bounded work for corrupt inputs. Record actual
   read operations/bytes, retries permitted by the frozen rules,
   elapsed time and refusal predicates. The SRR-013 sample timing is
   historical context. Seek population-scale budget evidence before
   approval.
3. Independently root the second pass and compare the required
   structural, input and result evidence. Stop cooperatively on operator
   cancellation, checkpoint or identity change, read denial, structural
   ambiguity, work-cap exhaustion or evidence durability failure.
   Preserve the durable prefix and seal successful or refused attempts.
   No automatic fresh launch or extra pair is included. Close the handle,
   have the operator exit normally, verify the exact epoch's exit and
   record postflight preservation independently from evidence validity.
4. Reproduce both acquisitions and comparison offline from sealed input.
   Retain private raw rows, count and fence evidence, refusal reasons and
   scoped manifests. Review redaction and redistribution before sharing
   any sanitized report. Have an independent challenger attempt to
   falsify both coverage and oracle-domain closure, including a
   false-positive match with an omitted owner.

The maximum conclusion from one fully qualifying pair is agreement of
the enumerated and modeled population in the inspected store at the
held checkpoint. Matched bracketing observations leave mutation between
reads, ABA, stable intermediate zeroes, generating invocation and
continuous lifetime unresolved.
