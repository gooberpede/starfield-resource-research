# SRR-012 lead register and evidence matrix

Dispositions apply to the scoped feasibility question on the
[pinned retail image](PROVENANCE.md#current-retail-identity). **Resolved**
means a bounded finding is supported. **Excluded** applies to the
examined candidate route. **Blocked** records a missing discriminator or
separately authorized next step. Each native address is a preferred VA.

| Lead | Examination and decision | Evidence or discriminator |
| --- | --- | --- |

The supported process-global root is relocatable and externally readable in principle. | `0x1423FF660/69F`, `0x1418993C6–0x1418993E3`, `0x140779676`, `0x14189754B`, `0x141882F42–0x141882F4E`. [Acquisition](SRR-012-acquisition-evidence.md) |

The matrix covers ownership, alternative access, output interpretation and
operational failure leads exposed by the supplied brief and checked native
sources. The bounded checks cannot establish an absence of alternative
engine paths or determine runtime invocation frequency.
