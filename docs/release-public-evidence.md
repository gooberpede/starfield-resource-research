**Inspection.** Both containers inflated and passed the tracked framing checks. Their save numbers, character names, two recorded version strings, 8 full, 21 light and 9 medium plugin names, and 26 flag-dependent plugin metadata records per save were recoverable. Original basenames also encode the respective character names. The private receipt retains the exact filenames, source paths, hashes and hit offsets. The neutral public aliases preserve every save byte and still expose the internal names. The release build suffix identifies the research release. Neither header fingerprints the executable that created the save. [Save identities and applicability](PROVENANCE.md#save2-and-save52-binary-evidence)

**Remaining clearance.** Screening covered the physical files and fully inflated streams, including ASCII and aligned/unaligned UTF-16LE patterns for identifiers, account-linked text, email-like strings and machine paths. Neither inflated save matched the one known Steam account in the tested representations. Both contained the same short email-shaped marker; Save52 also contained a short drive-path-shaped marker. These patterns alone do not identify a person or a workstation. The structural and signature checks identified plugin **references** and serialized state. They did not establish embedded distributable plugin files or clear all game-derived content. Arbitrary nested data, free text, player-authored labels and unparsed save units remain possible. The owner should inspect the recorded offsets, plugin metadata and any recognizable user-created content for each specimen, confirm that disclosure is acceptable to the affected player, and document a sharing basis for any embedded Bethesda or third-party material. Gameplay authorship and the research repository's license do not establish that permission. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)

**Assembly and fallback.** The payloads already exist at the measured hashes. The named release copies are planned. After separate clearance, copy the exact SFS bytes to the chosen neutral basenames and check their lengths and SHA-256 against the table. Keep the historical filenames, backup receipts, Steam metadata and local paths private. Preserve both originals pending the decisions. If either save is withheld, the method remains executable on other compatible lawful specimens. Readers can check published counts and record coordinates against the available specimen, and the selected screenshots independently show display states. Reproducing the historical two-save comparison requires both exact inputs; even with both, the pair cannot establish a controlled before/after action or a particular runtime restoration.
