## Scientific rules and bounded stopping conditions

- **Test alternatives rather than presume:** radial color could reflect per-resource survey credit, and Locations color appears scan-level dependent. Treat these as distinct hypotheses until the actual relevant UI/native predicates are joined. The Kreet screenshots strongly support different color rules, but do not by themselves prove the full native expression.
- **Credit and color can diverge.** A resource tile colored by scan level is not automatically surveyed. The screenshot brightness of full resource names is not a proven credit indicator. Handle rarity markers, full-name labels, selected-target tiles, and fallback RGB separately.
- **Atmosphere-only and overlap:** Volii Alpha has observed all-zero terrestrial `CTPerBiomeData` slots; model input lists atmospheric Benzene and Water. The observed `0/2`→`2/2` transition is interval-bounded from pre-landing map to landed-but-aboard state. It supports credit by landing completion, **not** a directly witnessed bit write or a proven exact touchdown callback. Kreet's atmospheric He-3 is distinct from its seven terrestrial entries in the tested model; reconcile any overlap rules only with actual evidence.
- **Scope:** static executable and installed interface decoding, offline existing dataset checks and screenshot inspection only. No live launch, debugger, Save2/Save52 parsing, live UI interaction, save modification, new internet dependency, generic game/UI-engine survey, algorithm alteration or broad native search without a grounded path. No unrelated startup allocator or serialization reopening.
- Follow each lead while it has a concrete locator and a discriminating question. Stop with **established**, **bounded exclusion**, or **unresolved named boundary** when grounded paths are exhausted; no arbitrary function-count, time or output-size quota. Do not promote call reachability into runtime execution.
