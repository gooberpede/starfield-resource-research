# SRR-009 — Retail debugger admission and Stage A observation

**Status:** Implementation brief — Phase 1 authorized for offline engineering only. **Stage A live debugger attachment is not authorized by this document.** The maintainer will decide whether to authorize it after reviewing the frozen Phase 1 delivery and independent challenge. Stage B (Save2 and Lodge) is outside this assignment and requires separate consideration and authorization.

## Controlling objective and handoff contract

Integrate the existing, disposable-qualified four-site hardware observer with the authenticated Steam-launched retail environment established in SRR-008. Produce a specific, independently qualified post-title Stage A configuration and operator run card. **Stop at the Phase 1 review gate.** If the maintainer subsequently issues a short written authorization identifying that exact reviewed snapshot, conduct the approved Stage A session under the *same SRR-009 investigation*, then perform postflight, seal evidence, obtain independent review and return an updated task-only patch.

Maintain separate dispositions:

- **Phase 1 engineering:** `READY FOR STAGE A APPROVAL`, `REWORK`, or `BLOCKED`. Readiness is not authority to attach.
- **Stage A scientific outcome:** `OBSERVED_REACHED_ALLOCATOR`, `OBSERVED_INVOKED`, `NOT_OBSERVED` within a qualified post-arming interval, or `INSTRUMENTATION_INCOMPLETE`; record compatible partial positives and bounded uncertainty. Report evidence integrity, observer health, target exit and protection outcomes separately. A qualified zero is not a negative for pre-title execution.
- **Overall handoff:** The independent challenger decides `COMPLETE`, `REWORK` or evidenced `BLOCKED` against the applicable criteria. An accepted Phase 1 ends at its frozen approval gate; it does not imply Stage A completion.

This work **must not** become a new general debugger framework, a retrospective whole-workspace preservation exercise, or an investigation of cache lifecycle and allocator semantics. Preserve the already established static model and previous review records.

## Phase 1 — Offline integration and qualification (authorized now)

### A. Preserve scope and choose a bounded admission policy

1. Record initial Git HEAD/index/status and the scoped exact dependencies, executable/tool identities, local evidence paths and currently applicable configuration. Do not mutate other repositories, historical dossiers, Ghidra databases, installed game files or existing saves. Use `.local-work/codex/SRR-009/`, `.local-work/independent-review/SRR-009/`, `.local-work/diffs/` and selected local evidence; do not turn transient outputs into perpetual protected inventory entries.
2. Convert SRR-008's authentic title-time evidence into a **reviewable risk-based module admission policy**. Pin critical bytes/identity and relevant mapped windows (especially main image and the system runtime actually used by the attach mechanism). Classify other observed Steam, NVIDIA and unrelated modules as expected or incidental with reasons. Unknown benign modules should be recorded and evaluated, not cause a reflexive shutdown; a change that defeats identity, exception ownership or coverage must block admission or qualify the affected interval. Distinguish stable backing-file identity from ASLR base, ordinary loader changes and loader-invisible activity. State what is and is not independently checked. Avoid pretending 146 identical modules are required at every instant.
3. Keep the environment as observed for the candidate Stage A run: interactive user's account, Steam URI `steam://rungameid/1716740`, Steam Cloud disabled, per-game Steam overlay disabled, NVIDIA overlay disabled, and SFSE not deployed/loaded. **Do not require globally disabling the Steam overlay** or an empty Saves directory. The observed overlay DLL is explicitly allowed only under the risk policy supported by evidence; record any relevant drift. No automatic environment cleanup or privilege escalation.

### B. Integrate the existing controller, not a replacement

4. Implement a retail-only, **default-refuse** admission path in the already qualified controller (or a minimal directly integrated launcher/adapter). The disposable fixture entry point must remain available and must not gain permission to target arbitrary PIDs. Associate the maintainer's title marker with one authenticated retail process handle, PID plus creation epoch, image backing path/bytes, same-session Steam attribution where available, and the stopped process. Revalidate the pinned executable, critical module identities, relocated pointers, mapped code windows, exception ownership and thread census **before arming**. Reject a changed process epoch or wrong image. Do not adopt unrelated successors by inference.
5. Use the existing reviewed post-title attach hardware-breakpoint method, including attribution of the debugger's attach break-in versus a competing breakpoint, four-slot per-thread ownership/readback, new-thread handling, RF/DR6 behavior, conservative evidence joins, per-window positive/negative coverage and incomplete classification. An event-pump failure must not silently convert to a clean zero. Reuse the durable journal/STOP prefix and non-killing recovery controls; do not expand to software INT3 tracing or install SFSE. Any new privilege or intervention required for this exact design is a documented approval blocker, not silent authority.
6. Establish an **operator-driven interface** for `TITLE_CONFIRMED`, `MAIN_MENU_CONFIRMED`, `STOP` and `QUIT_REQUESTED`/normal exit observation, with acknowledgment after the required evidence flush. The operator performs key/button advancement and Quit; Codex may initiate the approved Steam launch only **after Phase 2 authorization**. Marker times are operator-reported boundaries, not native UI transition frame timestamps. Support long pauses and delayed replies without a general experiment timeout. Avoid desktop UI automation.
7. Specify exact post-STOP behavior and debugger-exit policy: STOP ends new scientific eligibility; valid sealed evidence remains scientifically assessable if retirement or game exit fails. Continue qualified event servicing through normal Quit where feasible. Document a bounded, fixture-tested non-killing recovery strategy, exact-handle process ownership and its limits. Forced termination, unapproved detach, target modification and retries are **not** silently authorized. Do not require perfect target survival as an evidentiary prerequisite; do not equate crash tolerance with permission to crash the game intentionally.

## Phase 2 — Stage A live execution (conditional; NOT authorized yet)

It must cite the frozen Phase 1 manifest/binary and run card and explicitly select the permitted Steam launch, post-title debugger attachment, per-thread DR0–DR3 operations, process/memory reads, evidence destination, user-confirmed UI transitions, exit/recovery and any forced process intervention. Silence or approval of Phase 1 is not Stage A authorization. Revalidate identity and approved configuration at launch; if they drift, do not improvise.

If separately authorized:

1. Recheck installed game, interactive-user identity, settings, Steam state, protected saves and backups and both-drive floor; retain the normal populated save directories. Start one Starfield process via the authorized Steam URI under the maintainer's ordinary interactive account. No Save2, no new save, no Lodge crossing or gameplay research.
2. Wait for the operator's responsive title-card confirmation. Authenticate the exact Steam-launched process and title-time module environment, attach through the approved adapter, establish the actual attach break-in, thread census and complete four-slot arming/readback. Only then open the qualified observation interval. The title/menu confirmations must be bound to this process and instrumentation epoch. Startup before arming is explicitly **uncovered**.
3. The operator advances to the main menu, confirms it is responsive, and later issues STOP at an appropriate coherent state. Continue safely through unexpected but observable events within the approved scope; do not change sites or expand access opportunistically. Slow loading, a busy event pump and long operator intervals alone are not failures. Record relevant coverage gaps rather than falsely accepting a zero.
4. STOP closes scientific acquisition and drains/flushes a durable, hash-verified record prefix. The operator then quits normally. Process failure *after* a verified prefix is a separate operational outcome; preserve that prefix. Apply only the explicitly approved recovery authority and document every action and result. Do not reflexively terminate or restart Starfield. Reconcile the same process handle, event service and exit where possible.
5. Perform protected-input/save/backup and Cloud/postflight checks; retain original unsuccessful attempts and partial evidence. Analyze the actual four-site records and window completeness against SRR-005's causal join, with distinct observer health and scientific verdicts. The independent challenger checks original trace bytes and seals, correlation, gaps, operator markers, process identity and outcome classification. Challenge missed-event assumptions and reported negatives. Rework analysis or safely repeat offline validation within scope, but **a second retail launch requires new explicit authorization**.
6. Update the same SRR-009 dossier with actual Stage A evidence, limitations, review disposition and local evidence locators. Return a revised task-only diff and independent review for maintainer integration. Phase 2 can be scientifically complete with `NOT_OBSERVED` under a qualified window; it must not claim pre-title inactivity or full lifecycle/equivalence. No automatic commitment or push.
